Learn & Play

A free learning app for primary students (Class 1–5) in Sindh. Play quizzes, learn Urdu and Sindhi words, and chat with a friendly helper — in English, Urdu and Sindhi.

Install Learn & Play

Get the app icon on your screen. It opens like an app, even offline.

On a laptop or computer

  1. Open this website in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. Or open the ⋮ / … menu and choose “Install Learn & Play” (in Edge: Apps → Install this site as an app).
  3. Learn & Play gets its own icon on the desktop and Start menu, opens in its own window and works offline.
Or download the Windows app (.exe)

Works without internet. If Windows warns you, click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

On a phone

  1. Android (Chrome): open the ⋮ menu and tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  2. iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.
Or download the Android app (APK)

Open the file on the phone. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps”.
